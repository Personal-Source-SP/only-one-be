# Conversational English Coaching

## Overview

Use this skill during interactive chat turns (interviews, clarifications, planning reviews, debugging sessions) to help developers naturally elevate their professional engineering English.

## Workflow

1. In each conversational response turn, observe the user's input (especially if in Vietnamese or informal English).
2. At the footer of your response, provide a dedicated `💬 English Expression Coaching` section.
3. Offer 1–2 natural, idiomatically accurate ways a Senior Staff Engineer at a global tech company would phrase that thought, query, or decision.
4. Provide a brief explanation of why the phrasing is impactful (e.g., clarity, conciseness, diplomatic tone, precise technical vocabulary).
